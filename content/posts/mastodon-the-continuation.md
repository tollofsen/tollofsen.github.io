Seeing how little time I actually spend on social media, I decided to no longer bother with self hosting my Mastodon Instance. 

The effort of hosting the thing wasn't big at all, altough the disk space used for a single user instance was a lot larger then expected. Something that at times caused problems with the Oracle Free Tier ARM instance I was hosting the thing on. However, the amount of users on the instance has a way lower impact on the disk space used compared to the amount of instances you're federated with.

I'm now once again available at [mastodon.social](https://mastodon.social) under the handle [@jenstollofsen](https://mastodon.social/@jenstollofsen)
